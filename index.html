Lakshya A Agrawal

I am a Computer Science PhD student at UC Berkeley, where I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. Matei Zaharia and Prof. Dan Klein. I am affiliated with Sky Lab, BAIR, and Berkeley NLP.

I build AI systems that improve through experience, by optimizing their prompts, agent harnesses, and model weights. My research spans reflective optimization, reinforcement learning, and continual learning, alongside work on the reliability of generated code.

My background in programming languages shapes how I approach AI: studying how models, tools, and learning algorithms work together as a system. I am passionate about open source and turning research into useful software.
