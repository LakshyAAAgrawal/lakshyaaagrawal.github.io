About

By day I'm a computer science and AI researcher (more on my main site); this page is about the music. I do song covers on saxophone, and I've live-streamed my evening practice sessions on YouTube (and on Reddit's r/RedditSessions): exercises first, then songs from their sheets, often ones requested in the chat.

My first public performance was Skyfall at Open Mic Challenge 37 at My School of Rock, Bangalore, in March 2023. The photo above is from a spotlight session at Lahe Lahe.
